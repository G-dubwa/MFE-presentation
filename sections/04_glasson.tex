\begin{frame}{Three omitted Heston terms explain the proxy drift}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.24cm]{block body}
    \[
      \operatorname{drift}(g)
      =
      \underbrace{\kappa(\theta-v)g_v}_{\text{mean reversion}\times\text{variance sensitivity}}
      +
      \underbrace{\tfrac12\xi^2v\,g_{vv}}_{\text{vol-of-vol convexity}}
      +
      \underbrace{\rho\xi vS\,g_{Sv}}_{\text{spot--variance interaction}} .
    \]
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \vspace{0.28cm}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.31\textwidth}
      \centering
      \pill[Coral]{1}
      \vspace{0.1cm}

      {\small Apply Itô to \(g(S,v,t)=C_{\bs}(S,K_h,T_h-t,\sqrt v)\).}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.31\textwidth}
      \centering
      \pill[Gold]{2}
      \vspace{0.1cm}

      {\small Cancel only the terms covered by the frozen-volatility BS PDE.}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.31\textwidth}
      \centering
      \pill[Teal]{3}
      \vspace{0.1cm}

      {\small Three Heston terms remain, so drift is generally nonzero.}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
  \vspace{0.27cm}
  \risknote{The sign is not determined by \(\xi\) and \(\rho\) alone. Under the submitted parameters and simulated state distribution, the average residual was negative, matching the diagnostic.}

  \note{Walk through Itô, BS-PDE cancellation, then residual. Do not say correlation alone caused the negative drift.}

\begin{frame}{The evidence is strong within explicit boundaries}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.54\textwidth}
      \pill{What the evidence establishes}
      {\small\begin{enumerate}
        \item \textbf{Black--Scholes:} recovered the known finite-grid hedge.
        \item \textbf{Conditioning:} repaired the identified information failure.
        \item \textbf{Heston:} reduced terminal risk against the strongest tested heuristic once the liquid option was priced consistently.
      \end{enumerate}}
      \vspace{0.05cm}
      {\scriptsize\color{Slate}Learned option positions broadly follow variance-hedging direction, but soften at high variance under terminal MSE.}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.42\textwidth}
      \pill[Coral]{Where the conclusion stops}
      {\scriptsize\begin{tightitemize}
        \item three seeds and evaluation-path premium centering;
        \item comparators are local heuristics, not a derived finite-grid multi-instrument optimum;
        \item frictionless simulated Heston market;
        \item full-information headline; observable-market-information robustness is not stock-return-only filtering.
      \end{tightitemize}}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
  \vspace{0.08cm}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.18cm]{block body}
    \centering\color{DeepNavy}\bfseries
    A constrained, rigorously benchmarked neural policy can recover a known solution and adapt when the state and traded instrument set become more complex.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \sourcefoot{Submitted report, Secs.\ 4.1.4 and 5; Figure 4.3 interpretation and stated limitations.}
  \note{Close with the three calibrated claims. Do not claim neural networks should replace correct analytic hedges or that the Heston strategy is globally optimal.}
