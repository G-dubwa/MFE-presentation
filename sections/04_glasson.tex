\begin{frame}[t]{Why the frozen-volatility proxy drifts}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.11cm]{block body}
    \centering
    \small
    \textbf{Repriced proxy:}\quad
    \(g(t,S,v)=C_{\bs}\!\left(S,K_h,T_h-t,\sqrt v\right)\)
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \vspace{0.07cm}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.45\textwidth}
      \pill[Teal]{Cancelled by the frozen-\(v\) BS PDE}
      \vspace{0.05cm}
      \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.13cm]{block body}
        \centering\(\displaystyle g_t+rSg_S+\frac12vS^2g_{SS}-rg=0\)
      \end{beamercolorbox}
      \vspace{0.06cm}
      {\scriptsize Recomputing the BS level at each date does not make its path satisfy the Heston pricing equation.}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.51\textwidth}
      \pill[Coral]{Remaining because variance follows Heston}
      \vspace{0.05cm}
      \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.13cm]{block body}
        \centering\(\displaystyle
          R(t,S,v)=
          \kappa(\theta-v)g_v
          +\frac12\xi^2vg_{vv}
          +\rho\xi vSg_{Sv}\)
      \end{beamercolorbox}
      \vspace{0.05cm}
      {\scriptsize At submitted \(r=0\), discounted and undiscounted drift residuals coincide.}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
  \vspace{0.09cm}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.32\textwidth}
      \centering\pill[Teal]{1}\quad{\scriptsize mean reversion\(\times\)variance sensitivity}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.32\textwidth}
      \centering\pill[Gold]{2}\quad{\scriptsize volatility-of-volatility\(\times\)variance curvature}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.32\textwidth}
      \centering\pill[Coral]{3}\quad{\scriptsize spot--variance interaction}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
  \vspace{0.07cm}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.13cm]{block body}
    {\scriptsize\textbf{Scope:} The sign is state-dependent. Under the submitted parameters and simulated states, the average residual was negative, consistent with the diagnostic.}
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \sourcefoot{Submitted report, Sec.\ 4.1.4 and Appendix A.6.3.}
  \note{The proxy is recomputed using current variance, but the frozen-variance BS PDE cancels only the time, stock and discounting terms. Heston mean reversion, variance curvature and spot--variance interaction remain. Here \(v_t\) is variance and \(\sqrt{v_t}\) is volatility. Their combined sign depends on the state; under the submitted experiment it was negative.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Three conclusions survive the diagnostics}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.55\textwidth}
      \pill{What the experiments establish}
      \vspace{0.10cm}
      {\fontsize{8.5}{10.2}\selectfont
      \begin{enumerate}
        \setlength{\itemsep}{0.07cm}
        \item \textbf{Black--Scholes:} the neural strategy recovered the known finite-grid hedge.
        \item \textbf{Conditioning:} adding the missing contract information repaired the identified generalisation failure.
        \item \textbf{Heston:} with a consistently priced liquid option, the stock-and-option NN achieved lower terminal error than the strongest tested local heuristic in every submitted seed.
      \end{enumerate}}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.41\textwidth}
      \pill[Coral]{What they do not establish}
      \vspace{0.10cm}
      {\fontsize{8.5}{10.2}\selectfont
      \begin{tightitemize}
        \item more than three seeds or fully out-of-sample premium estimation;
        \item superiority to a derived finite-grid multi-instrument optimum;
        \item performance beyond the frictionless simulated Heston setting and submitted information assumptions.
      \end{tightitemize}}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
  \vspace{0.05cm}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.09cm]{block body}
    {\scriptsize\textbf{Observable-information check:} The ordering survived a causal EWMA variance proxy; the option quote remained contemporaneously observable and variance-informative.}
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \vspace{0.05cm}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,sep=0.12cm]{block body}
    \centering\color{DeepNavy}\bfseries
    A constrained and carefully validated neural policy can first recover a known solution and then adapt when the state dynamics, information set, and traded instruments become more complex.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \vspace{-0.03cm}
  \sourcefoot{Submitted report, Secs.\ 4.1.4 and 5; stated limitations and observable-information diagnostic.}
  \note{The bounded conclusion has three parts: recovery in Black--Scholes, repaired generalisation through conditioning, and lower submitted Heston terminal error than the strongest tested local heuristic. The limits are three seeds, test-path centring, local comparators, frictionless simulation and the submitted information assumptions. The EWMA diagnostic retained an informative option quote. End with the displayed sentence and stop.}
\end{frame}
